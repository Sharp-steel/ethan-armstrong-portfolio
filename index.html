My Projects

The Fellmonger

The Fellmonger is a 2D fantasy-themed boss-rush game that was created as a submission for McGameJam 2026. As the Fellmonger, you must hunt down evil monsters and control the fate of the world preventing insurmountable power levels.

Team Size: 5

Engine: Unity

My Responsibilities:

  • - Managing the scope of the project and team members
  • - Designing the overall aesthetic and theme
  • - Level and world design
  • - Planning & documentation

No Trespassing!

No Trespassing! is a small game created as a part of an academic project where the player explores an old abandoned office building. It is an experimental game that explores themes of urbex and enforced restriction versus exploration.

Team Size: Solo

Engine: Bitsy

My Responsibilities:

About Ethan Armstrong

After deliberating about my future for a long time, I finally discovered my passion for game design. I specialize in worldbuilding and narratives, and have used my prior design experience through graphic design and UI/UX design in order to create RPG style games, with an emphasis on fantasy, sci-fi and medieval themes that have a very unique feel to them, although I have experiemented with other genres as well.

Most of my personal work and projects are inspired from my culture, personal hobbies, and favourite games. Although I'm from Montreal, my English heritage has always allowed and inspired me to embrace fantasy, sci-fi and medieval culture within games. Most of the games that I make for this reason are usually consistent with these genres and subgenres.

Currently, I am a student at Concordia University in the Computation Arts Specialization program. I also hold prior experience as a graphic designer, graduating from the Graphic and Web Design program at John Abbott College.
